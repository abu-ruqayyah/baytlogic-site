### 1. Executive Summary

BaytLogic Technologies provides practical, hands-on technology training designed to help young people develop marketable technical skills, pursue employment opportunities, and build technology-based businesses.

We invite non-governmental organizations (NGOs), development agencies, philanthropic foundations, and Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives to partner with us in sponsoring youth for structured technical skills training. 

The core positioning of our programme is:

> **YOUTH TECHNOLOGY SKILLS → PRACTICAL EXPERIENCE → CERTIFICATION → EMPLOYMENT / ENTREPRENEURSHIP**

Our current practical specialisations focus on **Smart Home Automation** and **Professional CCTV Installation**, equipping participants with high-demand skills required across residential, commercial, and institutional sectors in Nigeria.

### 3. The Youth Skills Challenge

Across Nigeria, millions of young graduates and school leavers face high unemployment despite growing local demand for technology installation and maintenance services. 

Traditional educational programmes often focus heavily on theoretical concepts without providing practical access to modern hardware tools, smart devices, and network infrastructure.

BaytLogic addresses this challenge by providing direct, practical experience with real technology equipment, preparing participants to enter employment pathways or launch independent technical service businesses.

### 6. Practical Training Methodology

BaytLogic emphasizes real-world application over classroom lecturing:

* **Real Equipment:** Participants work directly with physical IP cameras, NVRs, smart switches, Zigbee gateways, PoE switches, and crimping tools.
* **Branded Hardware Standard:** Training utilizes established commercial equipment from leading manufacturers such as **EZVIZ, Reolink, and Hikvision**.
* **Hands-on Installation Exercises:** Trainees perform physical cable runs, connector crimping, router IP configurations, and mobile app pairing.
* **Fault Troubleshooting:** Participants practice diagnosing network connectivity errors, power drops, and configuration mismatches.
* **Assessment & Evaluation:** Certification requires passing a practical hands-on installation assessment.
