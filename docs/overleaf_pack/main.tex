\section{INTRODUCTION}
BaytLogic Technologies is pleased to present this proposal to Hamjik Care Initiative for the delivery of an intensive, practical technical skills development programme focused on CCTV Installation, Networking, Configuration, Maintenance and Smart Home Automation.

The proposed programme is designed to equip \textbf{40 young people} with practical, market-relevant technology skills that can contribute to employability, self-employment, entrepreneurship and sustainable livelihood opportunities.

Rather than relying primarily on classroom lectures, the programme adopts a strongly practical approach in which participants will work directly with CCTV systems, networking tools, cables, connectors, NVR equipment, smart-home devices and automation technologies.

Participants will learn how to install, configure, test, troubleshoot and deploy real technology systems, while also receiving introductory entrepreneurship training on how such skills can be converted into professional services.

The programme will be delivered by \textbf{BaytLogic Technologies}, a Nigerian technology company specializing in CCTV and security systems, smart home automation, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, networking, software development and practical technology training.

% SECTION 4: PROGRAMME RATIONALE
% ==============================================================================
\section{PROGRAMME RATIONALE}
Technology-driven security and automation services are becoming increasingly important across homes, schools, offices, businesses, hotels, healthcare facilities, religious institutions and residential estates.

At the same time, many young people possess limited access to hands-on technical training and professional tools that would enable them to enter these emerging markets.

CCTV installation and smart-home automation represent particularly practical areas because they combine:
\begin{itemize}[label=\checkicon]
    \item Networking \& Structured Cabling
    \item Electrical / electronic fundamentals
    \item IoT (Internet of Things) technologies
    \item Mobile applications \& Remote monitoring
    \item Physical security systems
    \item Hardware installation \& Mounting
    \item Technical Troubleshooting
    \item Customer service \& Project estimation
    \item Entrepreneurship \& Business development
\end{itemize}

The proposed programme therefore seeks to bridge the gap between learning about technology and actually being able to deploy technology.

\section{PROGRAMME OBJECTIVES}
The programme aims to:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Equip participants with foundational knowledge of CCTV surveillance systems.
    \item Develop practical skills in CCTV installation and configuration.
    \item Train participants in CAT6 cable termination and network cable testing.
    \item Introduce participants to IP networking principles relevant to CCTV systems.
    \item Develop participants' ability to configure NVR-based surveillance systems.
    \item Teach participants basic CCTV troubleshooting and fault diagnosis.
    \item Introduce participants to modern smart-home and IoT technologies.
    \item Enable participants to configure smart lighting, sensors and automated curtains.
    \item Demonstrate smartphone and voice-controlled automation.
    \item Develop participants' practical problem-solving abilities.
    \item Introduce participants to technical service pricing, client acquisition and entrepreneurship.
    \item Provide each participant with basic technical tools to support continued practice after the programme.
    \item Assess participants' practical competencies at the end of the programme.
    \item Provide successful participants with certificates documenting completion of the training.
\end{enumerate}

% ==============================================================================
% SECTION 7: THREE-DAY TRAINING CURRICULUM
% ==============================================================================
\section{THREE-DAY TRAINING CURRICULUM}

% --- DAY 1 ---
\subsection{DAY 1 — CCTV Fundamentals, Networking \& Cable Termination}

\subsubsection{Module 1: Introduction to CCTV Technology}
\textbf{Participants will learn:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item What CCTV is and how surveillance systems work
    \item CCTV system architecture
    \item Analog vs IP CCTV
    \item Camera types and applications (Dome, Bullet, PTZ, Indoor/Outdoor)
    \item Camera resolution, Night vision/Infrared, and Field of view
    \item Storage requirements calculation
    \item NVR and DVR technologies
    \item PoE (Power over Ethernet) and network infrastructure
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{tcolorbox}[calloutbox]
\textbf{Practical Laboratory 1 — CCTV Equipment Identification}\\
\textit{Participants will:} Identify CCTV cameras, NVR components, storage devices, PoE equipment, network cables, connectors, and installation tools, and design a basic CCTV system layout.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsubsection{Module 2: Networking \& Structured Cabling}
\textbf{Participants will learn:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Basic networking concepts and Ethernet networking
    \item CAT5e and CAT6 cables structure
    \item RJ45 connectors and T568A / T568B wiring standards
    \item Straight-through network cables vs Crossover cables
    \item Cable routing and management best practices
    \item Basic network troubleshooting
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{tcolorbox}[calloutbox]
\textbf{Practical Laboratory 2 — CAT6 Termination}\\
\textit{Participants will individually practise:} Measuring cable, stripping CAT6 cable, arranging cable conductors, preparing RJ45 connectors, crimping RJ45 connectors, testing network cables with LAN tester, identifying wiring faults, and correcting failed terminations.\\
\textbf{Note:} This practical session ensures that every participant has direct experience with one of the most fundamental skills required in CCTV and networking installations.
\end{tcolorbox}

% --- DAY 2 ---
\subsection{DAY 2 — CCTV Installation, Configuration \& Smart Home Automation}

\subsubsection{Module 3: CCTV Installation \& NVR Configuration}
\textbf{Participants will learn:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item CCTV installation planning, camera positioning, mounting, and cable routing
    \item Power requirements and PoE networking
    \item IP addressing scheme and NVR configuration
    \item Camera discovery, channel mapping, recording configuration, and playback
    \item User management, remote monitoring, and mobile CCTV applications
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{tcolorbox}[calloutbox]
\textbf{Practical Laboratory 3 — CCTV System Deployment}\\
\textit{Participants will work in teams to:} Connect CCTV cameras to NVR, configure network connections, configure camera channels and recording, test live viewing, review recorded footage, and configure remote/mobile viewing.
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{tcolorbox}[calloutbox]
\textbf{Practical Troubleshooting Exercise}\\
\textit{Participants will diagnose simulated problems including:} Camera offline, no video signal, faulty network cable, incorrect IP configuration, power failure, and storage/recording problems.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsubsection{Module 4: Introduction to Smart Home Automation}
\textbf{Participants will learn:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item What is smart home automation? Internet of Things (IoT) fundamentals
    \item Smart home architecture (Wi-Fi vs Zigbee devices, Smart hubs/gateways)
    \item Smart switches, bulbs, plugs, motion sensors, door/window sensors, smart curtains
    \item Mobile app control, voice control integration, and automation routines
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{tcolorbox}[calloutbox]
\textbf{Practical Laboratory 4 — Smart Home Configuration}\\
\textit{Participants will work with practical smart-home devices to demonstrate:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Smart Lighting:} Smartphone $\rightarrow$ Light ON/OFF
    \item \textbf{Voice Control:} Voice Command $\rightarrow$ Light ON/OFF
    \item \textbf{Door Automation:} Door Opens $\rightarrow$ Light Activates
    \item \textbf{Motion Automation:} Motion Detected $\rightarrow$ Light Activates
    \item \textbf{Smart Curtain:} Smartphone $\rightarrow$ Curtain Opens/Closes
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}

% --- DAY 3 ---
\subsection{DAY 3 — Integrated Project, Troubleshooting \& Entrepreneurship}

\subsubsection{Module 5: Integrated Practical Project}
Participants will be divided into practical teams. Each team will be given a realistic client scenario requiring them to plan and deploy a small technology solution.

Depending on available equipment, projects will combine CCTV, NVR, Networking, Smart lighting, Sensors, Smart curtain, and Mobile control.

\textbf{Participants will be expected to:}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Understand the client requirement
    \item Select appropriate equipment & plan the installation
    \item Design the basic system layout
    \item Terminate required CAT6 cables
    \item Connect and configure the equipment & test the system
    \item Identify and resolve simulated faults
    \item Demonstrate the completed working solution to instructors
\end{enumerate}
